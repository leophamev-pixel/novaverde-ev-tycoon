Privacy Policy — NovaVerde EV Tycoon

App Bundle ID / Package Name: com.novaverde.evtycoon

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Authentication Data: When you sign in using Sign in with Apple, Google Sign-In, or play as a Guest (powered by Firebase Authentication), we store your unique user identifier (UID), display name, and optional email address solely to synchronize your Cloud Save and Leaderboard rank across devices.
  • Gameplay & Leaderboard Data: We store your in-game progress (owned EV charging lots, charger tiers, GreenDollar G$ balance, GreenCoin GC balance, Net Worth, and 1x real-time anti-cheat verification hash) to display rankings on the global and regional leaderboards.
  • Advertising Data (Google AdMob): Our game uses Google AdMob to display optional Rewarded Video Ads and Banner Ads. On iOS, we request permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before accessing your device's advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit / Google Play Billing / RevenueCat): All financial transactions are processed directly by Apple App Store or Google Play Store. We do not collect or store your credit card or banking details; we only receive anonymous purchase receipts to grant your GreenCoin (GC) or Remove Ads entitlement.

2. Account & Data Deletion Request

You can delete your local and cloud game data at any time directly inside the game via ⚙️ Settings ➔ 💾 Save & Cloud ➔ 🗑️ Reset / Delete Account Data, or by contacting our support email. Upon deletion, your UID, Cloud Save snapshot, and Leaderboard entry are permanently erased within 7 days.

3. Children's Privacy

NovaVerde EV Tycoon is a general-audience business simulation game and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or need support with your purchases, please contact: support@novaverde-game.com.
